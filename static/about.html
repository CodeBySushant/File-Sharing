Our Journey

How ShareYou came to life

Hey, I'm Sushant Sharma — a B.Tech final-year student at the National Institute of Technology, Bhopal, studying Computer Science Engineering. ShareYou started as a project assigned by our professor, and like most great things, it grew beyond its original scope.

Instead of building yet another plain file-uploader, I decided to channel everything I'd learned — backend architecture, clean UI design, real-time systems — into something that actually felt worth using. The goal was simple: make file sharing feel as smooth and modern as the tools we use every day, like Notion, Linear, or Figma.

What you're seeing now is the result of that vision: a platform built with care, curiosity, and a genuine belief that student projects don't have to look like student projects.
